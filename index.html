The Atelier

Crafted by hand, worn for a lifetime.

AURA was founded on a rejection of speed — of collections designed to be discarded after a single season. Every piece begins in our atelier, where a small team of pattern-makers and tailors work from first sketch to final stitch.

We work with a small circle of family-run mills across Italy and Japan, chosen for cloth that improves with age rather than wearing out. The result is a wardrobe meant to be lived in for years, not months.
